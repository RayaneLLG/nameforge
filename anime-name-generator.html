How to Choose the Perfect Fantasy Name

A name carries the weight of a character's ancestry, culture, and power. Whether you are building world-building lore or rolling up a new D&D character, picking a fitting name grounds your concept in the fantasy realm.

🧝‍♂️ Elven Names

Elven names prioritize fluid, melodic vowels, soft sibilants, and references to nature, starlight, and ancient forests.

🧔 Dwarven Names

Dwarven names feature heavy, resonant consonants (K, Th, R, Gr) reflecting stone, iron, subterranean strongholds, and ancestral honor.

🧙‍♂️ Wizard & Mage Names

Mystical names often carry ancient or classical roots, paired with epithets that denote elemental mastery or arcane scholarly achievements.

🐉 Dragon Names

Dragons command powerful, guttural, or sharp names that echo ancient languages, flame, and primordial scale.
