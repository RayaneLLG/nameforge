What is a fantasy name generator?

A fantasy name generator is a creative tool designed to help you discover original-sounding names for fictional characters, creatures, kingdoms and entire fantasy worlds. Instead of spending hours trying to invent the right combination of sounds, NameForge gives you a collection of ideas in seconds.

A good fantasy name is more than a random collection of letters. It can suggest a character's personality, origin, culture, status, history or role within a fictional world.

Built for characters and worldbuilding

NameForge's Fantasy Name Generator is designed for writers, gamers, RPG players, developers, role-playing communities and anyone creating a fictional universe. Use the generated names as inspiration for protagonists, antagonists, warriors, mages, rulers, ancient beings and many other fantasy characters.

Choose the style that matches your character

Hero names are designed to feel adventurous and legendary. Warrior names emphasize strength and battle. Mage names have a mystical and arcane feeling. Dark Fantasy names are suited to mysterious characters and antagonists. Royal names are designed for rulers and noble families. Ancient names evoke forgotten civilizations, immortal beings and legendary sages.

Control the length of your names

Choose between short, medium and long names. Short names are useful when you want something simple and memorable. Medium names provide a balanced fantasy style, while long names can work particularly well for powerful or ancient characters.

Generate, compare and save your favorites

Every generation gives you multiple possibilities. When you find something you like, you can Save it for later, Like it as a favorite, add a personal Note, or Copy the name.
