What is NameForge?

NameForge is a collection of specialized name generators created to help you turn ideas into memorable characters, worlds and stories. Instead of spending hours trying to invent the right combination of sounds, explore different naming styles and quickly discover possibilities that match your creation.

A name is often one of the first things people remember about a fictional character or world. A heroic name can suggest courage. A darker name can create mystery. A royal name can communicate history and power.

Whether you are writing a novel, creating an RPG campaign, designing a game, inventing an anime-inspired character or building an entire fantasy universe, the goal is the same: help you find a name that feels right.

The generators are designed to encourage experimentation. A generated name does not have to be the final answer. It can be the starting point for a character, kingdom, faction, creature, username or entire story.
