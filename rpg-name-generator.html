An RPG name generator is a tool that helps players, writers and game creators quickly find names for role-playing game characters.

Whether you are creating a powerful warrior, a mysterious mage, a sneaky rogue or a legendary paladin, finding the right name can make your character feel more memorable.

RPG warrior names

Warriors are powerful frontline characters. Their names can suggest strength, courage, honor and determination.

Mages and spellcasters often need names with a mysterious or magical feeling. Generate names for wizards, sorcerers and other magic users.
